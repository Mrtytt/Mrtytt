About

I am the Founder & CTO of Cody, working on AI-assisted enterprise software, configurable business platforms and full-stack product architecture.

My work spans the complete engineering lifecycle:

translating business requirements into technical systems,

designing application and database architecture,

developing backend and frontend platforms,

building authorization and automation infrastructure,

and turning complex operational processes into maintainable software products.

I am particularly interested in systems that reduce hardcoded development through metadata, reusable runtime components and controlled AI-assisted automation.

Building Cody

Cody is an AI-powered product-building platform designed to help teams transform ideas and business processes into working software systems.

My current engineering focus includes:

AI-assisted product requirement analysis

Metadata-first application architecture

Dynamic table, form and grid generation

Business action and lifecycle orchestration

Workflow and automation engines

Role-based access and permission systems

Multi-tenant enterprise architecture

Configurable ERP and SaaS applications

Runtime workspaces for business operations

Controlled, auditable AI execution

The objective is not simply to generate code, but to create a reliable platform that can understand requirements, design systems and apply changes through deterministic and governed backend processes.

What I Work On

AI-Assisted Product Engineering

Designing systems where language models analyze requirements and produce structured product plans, while deterministic backend services remain responsible for validation, authorization, execution, auditing and rollback.

Metadata-First Enterprise Platforms

Building platforms where entities, fields, relations, forms, grids, actions, permissions, reports and workflows are centrally defined rather than repeatedly hardcoded for every product or customer.

Business Action Frameworks

Developing controlled action systems for approvals, lifecycle transitions, record operations, validations, document generation and other business processes.

Dynamic Runtime Experiences

Creating reusable Workspace components capable of rendering business applications from metadata, including searchable grids, forms, lookups, actions and permission-aware interfaces.

Workflow and Automation Systems

Designing safe workflow foundations with versioning, publishing, execution history, conditional branching, idempotency and controlled side effects.

Engineering Principles

Clarity over unnecessary complexity.

Architecture before implementation.

Business rules belong in trusted backend services.

Metadata should remain a consistent source of truth.

AI should plan and assist; deterministic systems should execute.

Automation must be controlled, observable and auditable.

User interfaces should hide technical complexity.

Production reliability matters more than demo shortcuts.
